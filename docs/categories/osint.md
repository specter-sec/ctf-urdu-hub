# OSINT

OSINT ka matlab publicly available sources ko responsible tareeqe se analyze karna hai. Public ka matlab automatically safe-to-collect ya accurate nahi; consent, purpose limitation, identity risk aur source reliability ko pehle socho.

## Safe beginner exercise

[Metadata Ka Nishan](../writeups/osint/metadata-ka-nishan.md) ka fictional JSON + image pair banao. JSON ke invented claims ko padho, phir unhein independently verify na kar sake to “unverified” label karo. Is lab mein koi real person, real image metadata, ya web lookup nahi. [Generator source](https://github.com/specter-sec/ctf-urdu-hub/blob/main/labs/osint-metadata.py) offline hai.

## Aage parho

- [Bellingcat: Online Investigation Toolkit](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit)
- [ExifTool documentation](https://exiftool.org/)
- [Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations](https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/projects/berkeley-protocol/)

Kisi private shakhs ko identify, track, contact, ya expose karne ke liye research na karo. [Ethics & Safe Practice](../ethics.md) aur applicable law follow karo.
